## 5. 2026 IRS numbers (Notice 2025-67)
- **IRA:** $7,500, plus $1,100 catch-up at 50+ (total $8,600).
- **401(k)/403(b)/gov 457/TSP:** $24,500.
  - Catch-up at 50+: $8,000 (total $32,500).
  - Ages 60-63: catch-up $11,250 instead (total $35,750).
- **SIMPLE:** $17,000. **Plan annual additions limit:** $72,000.
- **Roth IRA phase-outs:** single $153k-168k; married filing jointly $242k-252k; married filing separately $0-10k.
- **RMDs:**
  - Age 73 if born 1951-59; age 75 if born 1960+.
  - Roth IRA owners have no RMDs. Roth 401(k) RMDs were removed from 2024 (SECURE 2.0).
- **Early withdrawal:** 10% additional tax before 59½ (IRC 72(t)).
- **Rollover rules:**
  - Only one 60-day IRA-to-IRA rollover per 12 months, counted across all IRAs (Bobrow; IRS since 2015).
  - Trustee-to-trustee transfers and direct rollovers are unlimited.
  - An eligible-rollover distribution paid to you from an employer plan has 20% mandatory withholding.
  - The 60-day deadline can sometimes be waived (Rev. Proc. 2020-46 self-certification).
- **SIMPLE IRA:** 25% penalty if withdrawn or rolled to a non-SIMPLE account in the first 2 years of participation.
- **Collectibles tax:** gains on physical metal outside an IRA, and on grantor-trust ETFs like GLD, are taxed up to 28% long-term.
- **Inherited IRAs:** non-spouse beneficiaries cannot roll over; they can only do a trustee-to-trustee transfer to an inherited IRA. The 10-year rule applies to most non-spouse beneficiaries.
- **Pre-tax to Roth conversion:** taxable in the conversion year.
- **IRC 408(m):**
  - Collectibles (including most coins and metals) held in an IRA are treated as distributions.
  - Exceptions: certain US coins (Gold/Silver/Platinum American Eagles, state-issued coins) and bullion meeting CFTC-contract fineness, in the physical possession of the trustee.
  - Fineness: gold .995 (Eagle exempt by statute), silver .999, platinum .9995, palladium .9995.
  - Not eligible: Krugerrands, most proof/numismatic coins (except American Eagle proofs, which the statute covers), collectible coins, gold jewelry.
- **McNulty v. Commissioner, 157 T.C. No. 10 (2021):** home storage via an "IRA LLC" counted as a taxable distribution plus penalties.

## 8. SERP and E-E-A-T notes
- **Who ranks now:** results are crowded with borrowed-domain pages (clutejournals, hblr, pressbooks), paid press releases and spun spam. Google's site-reputation-abuse enforcement is ongoing.
- **What works:**
  - Original worked examples and tables.
  - Primary-source citations: IRS Pub 590-A/B, IRC 408(m), Notice 2025-67, CFTC/SEC releases.
  - Balanced downsides: no yield, the drawdowns, fee drag.
  - Dated figures; no "guaranteed" or "safe haven immune" language.
- **Caveats:** recommend a fee-only adviser or tax professional, and keep the not-advice disclaimer.
